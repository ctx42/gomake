# Code of Conduct

This project is a technical community. The goal of this document is simple: keep
interactions professional and focused on the work so that anyone can
participate and contribute effectively.

## Expected behavior

- Stay on topic. Keep discussion focused on GoMake, its code, and its use.
- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive, technical feedback.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on. Mistakes are normal.
- Respect the maintainers' decisions about scope, direction, and merges.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks against any participant.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Publishing another person's private information without their consent.
- Deliberately derailing the project with off-topic or bad-faith content.
- Spam, including unsolicited advertising or repeated irrelevant posts.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages, and any other official channel — and to conduct in those spaces that
affects the project or its participants.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate it, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or ban
participants for repeated or serious violations. Enforcement decisions are at
the maintainers' discretion and will be applied consistently.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private
reporting: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a
vulnerability**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled discreetly. The
privacy of anyone reporting an issue will be respected.
